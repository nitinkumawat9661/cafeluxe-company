# TrustFirst Solutions Competitor SEO, AEO and GEO Research

Research date: 2026-08-07

This document records the competitor patterns used to improve the TrustFirst Solutions website. The implementation uses original copy, original page structure and openly licensed fonts. No competitor font files, images, source code or distinctive page layout were copied.

## Crawl limitations

- Softonexa and FlowPxl did not return usable homepage content to the crawler during this research pass.
- Social Sparrow's homepage was initially unavailable through direct open, but indexed service pages were available and reviewed.
- Findings from unavailable pages were not invented or used as factual inputs.

## Competitor patterns worth using

### Sinfode

- Strong local Sikar targeting in headings and long-form FAQ content.
- Broad topical coverage through dedicated course and location pages.
- Trust-building sections using process, practical work, testimonials and accreditation.

TrustFirst action:
- Use location-specific service language without repeating the same exact-match phrase unnaturally.
- Expand direct-answer FAQs and internal links.

### Sikar Infotech

- Clear service taxonomy covering marketing, web, apps, ecommerce and branding.
- Local trust language, portfolio, process, testimonials and frequently updated blog posts.
- Strong "IT and digital marketing company in Sikar" entity positioning.

TrustFirst action:
- Strengthen service entities, internal service links, process explanations and local business context.
- Avoid unsupported "best" claims and invented project statistics.

### Rajasthan Future Tech

- Exact local service headings and a compact service/portfolio/testimonial structure.
- Strong use of Sikar and Rajasthan in page titles and service descriptions.

TrustFirst action:
- Create one authoritative Sikar landing page instead of keyword stuffing every page.
- Keep claims evidence-based.

### My Digital Desh

- Large service footprint including web design, SEO, PPC, social media, video and reputation management.
- Strong commercial intent through pricing and quotation calls to action.

TrustFirst action:
- Cover performance marketing, website development, content and reputation-building terminology where it matches actual services.
- Keep pricing contextual instead of publishing an arbitrary range.

### Social Sparrow

- Detailed social media, content creation, photography, videography, community management and campaign service descriptions.
- Strong local entity information and dedicated service pages.

TrustFirst action:
- Expand social media and content language around strategy, platform-specific content, community response and performance measurement.
- Do not claim full photography or videography production unless it becomes an approved TrustFirst service.

### Pro Indies

- Outcome-led hero copy, clear service grouping and compact credibility metrics.
- Strong case-study structure connecting work to measurable outcomes.
- Clear, modern editorial hierarchy.

TrustFirst action:
- Use outcome-led copy and structured service cards.
- Publish only verified metrics and case studies.

### Splash Digital

- Strong coverage of performance marketing, lead generation, WhatsApp marketing, automation and API integration.
- Direct growth-oriented hero and repeated conversion calls to action.

TrustFirst action:
- Add performance marketing, WhatsApp lead flow and follow-up terminology.
- Keep ad spend and service fees clearly separated.

### UnixWave

- Strong modern technology positioning, numbered four-step process, detailed service capabilities and concise FAQs.
- Bold display typography, dark surfaces, bright accent treatment and structured capability lists.

TrustFirst action:
- Use an original numbered process, compact capability strip and stronger geometric display typography.
- Use Space Grotesk and Manrope through `next/font/google` as openly licensed alternatives. No UnixWave font asset was copied.

### Dfyte

- Very deep niche taxonomy, long-form question coverage and specialized service groupings.
- Political consulting content is not relevant to TrustFirst's current public positioning.

TrustFirst action:
- Use the topical-depth lesson only: explain actual services with clear subtopics and FAQs.
- Do not introduce political campaign services or unrelated keywords.

### WebSikar

- Relevant local service themes around digital marketing, web development, design and video.
- Useful as a local keyword reference, but not as a copy source.

TrustFirst action:
- Use original local landing-page copy and service entities.

## AEO and GEO implementation

- Direct-answer content on the Sikar landing page.
- Expanded question-and-answer coverage using factual, concise language.
- FAQPage structured data.
- ProfessionalService, LocalBusiness, Organization, Service, WebSite and WebPage entities.
- Explicit service areas, contact point, language support and service catalog.
- `llms.txt` with canonical facts and important URLs.
- Internal links to relevant service pages.

## Visual implementation

- Preserved TrustFirst's black, charcoal and gold visual identity.
- Added open-source Manrope body typography and Space Grotesk display typography.
- Added an original capability strip, numbered service cards, four-stage process and diagnostic growth-gap section.
- No competitor images, logos, font files, CSS or page markup were copied.
